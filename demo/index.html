Try the whole journey (about 5 minutes)
  1. Open the app and search as a guest. Try the map and the filters.
  2. Open a home and tap Sign up to see full details. Use any new number, e.g. 01700000555. The code arrives as a demo SMS.
  3. Verify your ID with the demo pictures, then approve it in the admin website (ID checks).
  4. Message a landlord and suggest viewing times. Demo landlords reply by themselves.
  5. Tap Share my details: you both get the same record with its fingerprint.
  6. Confirm the deal with an OTP and choose the protected advance. Tap I've sent the transfer.
  7. In the admin website, Advances and bank → Import today's statement. The advance is now held.
  8. Back in the app, do the move-in check. In the admin website, prepare the payout as one founder, then Switch founder and approve it.

On one phone only? Open the DEMO tab on the right edge of the app and turn on auto-approve.

The app and the admin website share data in this browser. To use a phone and a laptop together, run node server.js in this folder and open the address it prints on both. Maps load from OpenFreeMap when online and fall back to a drawn map offline.
